# PART 1 — FINAL DIRECTOR'S SCREENPLAY

**K.G.F CHAPTER 3 — THE GLOBAL EMPIRE**
60-second intro · Unofficial fan concept · Period: early 1980s

## 1.1 Director's review — what changed and why

The story, characters, key dialogue and the final Rocky reveal are the same as in your draft. I changed timing and continuity only where the draft would break in practice.

| # | Problem in the draft | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Title card had only 1 second.** "K.G.F / CHAPTER 3 / THE GLOBAL EMPIRE / UNOFFICIAL FAN CONCEPT" can't be read in 1 s, so the payoff of the whole teaser would be lost. | The title now gets **3.0 s** (0:57–1:00). I found the time by trimming Shot 1, Shot 5 and the montage by 0.5–1 s each. |
| 2 | **The radio line couldn't fit in Shot 3.** At news pace the Malayalam line takes about 4.3 s, and Shot 3 was 4 s with a zoom. | The line becomes a **J-cut**: it starts at 0:05.5, under the end of the storm. |
| 3 | **Shot 2 was really two shots.** An aerial storm and gold sinking underwater can't come from one AI clip: they need different cameras and different physics. | Split into **2A** (aerial ship) and **2B** (underwater gold). Same story beat. |
| 4 | **The gold-ring logic had a gap.** Enemies put Rocky's "R" ring on a table (Shot 11), and later Rocky is wearing "the same ring" (Shot 19). How did he get it back? | The enemies' ring now carries a **salvage tag: "RECOVERED — WRECK SITE"**. It's their proof that he's dead. When Rocky wears the identical ring in Shot 19, it means **the ring they recovered was planted**. The gap becomes the twist. |
| 5 | **The stamp-to-gate sound bridge needed something to show.** Shot 9's gates are broken and abandoned, so nothing on screen "closes". | Added **8B**, a 1-second insert of a rusted KGF iron gate slamming shut. The THUD becomes a CLANG, and then we cut to the abandoned mine. |
| 6 | **The narrator lines in Scene 6 were rushed.** Each line had 2 s; it needs about 2.3 s. | Shots 18 and 19 are 2.5 s and 3.0 s, and both lines pre-lap slightly. |
| 7 | **Officer's whisper on camera.** The Shot 17 extreme close-up is on a document, so a whisper on camera would need lip-sync on an insert shot. | He whispers **off-screen** over the telex insert. That's one fewer lip-sync shot and makes it creepier. |
| 8 | **The BGM hit was competing with Rocky's line.** | After "ലോകം." **all sound drops out for 0.3 s** (a suck-out), then the BOOM lands on the cut to the title. |
| 9 | **Real-person likeness.** The teaser must not reproduce any real actor's or politician's face. | Every character reference in Part 3 is an original face. Costume and silhouette carry the identity: Rocky's long hair, beard and black coat. |

**Shot count:** 21 story shots plus 2 split inserts (2B, 8B) = **23 generated clips**.

## 1.2 Final timed screenplay (60.0 s)

### SCENE 1 — THE END OF AN EMPIRE · 00:00.0–00:10.5 · Indian Ocean · Night

**SHOT 1 · 00:00.0–00:02.5 (2.5 s)**
Black screen. *Tak… tak… tak…* An old typewriter. Letters strike onto aged paper, one at a time:
**CLASSIFIED — K.G.F.**

**SHOT 2A · 00:02.5–00:05.0 (2.5 s)**
Extreme wide aerial view. A violent storm on the Indian Ocean. A lightning flash shows a huge cargo ship tilted 30°, its bow going under.

**SHOT 2B · 00:05.0–00:06.5 (1.5 s)**
Underwater. Gold bars tumble slowly down into black water, catching the last flicker of lightning from above.
*(Radio VO begins at 0:05.5, J-cut.)*

**SHOT 3 · 00:06.5–00:10.5 (4.0 s)**
An old valve radio on a wooden shelf. The camera pushes in slowly toward the glowing dial.
NEWSREADER (radio, 0:05.5–0:09.8):
> "ഇന്ത്യൻ മഹാസമുദ്രത്തിൽ റോക്കിയുടെ കപ്പൽ മുങ്ങി. റോക്കി കൊല്ലപ്പെട്ടതായാണ് റിപ്പോർട്ടുകൾ!"

Radio static swells. **CUT TO BLACK at 0:10.0**, with 0.5 s of black and static fading out.

### SCENE 2 — THE NARRATOR · 00:10.5–00:18.0 · Ingalagi's Study

**SHOT 4 · 00:10.5–00:14.0 (3.5 s)**
Top-down view of a desk: a photograph of Rocky, KGF files, yellowed newspapers. An old man's hand closes a manuscript whose last page reads **THE END**.
NARRATOR (V.O., 0:10.8–0:14.2):
> "ഒരു കപ്പൽ മുങ്ങി… ഒരു സാമ്രാജ്യം അവസാനിച്ചു…"

**SHOT 5 · 00:14.0–00:18.0 (4.0 s)**
Side-profile close-up. The narrator slowly takes off his spectacles.
NARRATOR (0:14.8–0:17.0):
> "അങ്ങനെയാണ്… ലോകം വിശ്വസിച്ചത്."

1 s of silence. Only a wall clock ticks.

### SCENE 3 — THE FALSE VICTORY · 00:18.0–00:27.0 · Prime Minister's Office, Delhi

**SHOT 6 · 00:18.0–00:21.0 (3.0 s)**
Wide shot. Ramika Sen stands at a tall window with her back to us. An intelligence officer enters from the right.
OFFICER (0:19.0–0:20.7): *"Madam, the operation is complete."*

**SHOT 7 · 00:21.0–00:24.5 (3.5 s)**
Close-up. Ramika looks down at Rocky's photograph on the desk.
RAMIKA SEN (0:21.7–0:22.9): *"Close the KGF file."* (beat) (0:23.5–0:24.1): *"Forever."*

**SHOT 8 · 00:24.5–00:26.0 (1.5 s)**
Extreme close-up. A red rubber stamp comes down on the file cover: **CLOSED**. **THUD!** (0:25.2)

**SHOT 8B · 00:26.0–00:27.0 (1.0 s)** *(new insert)*
A rusted iron gate at KGF slams shut, throwing dust. **CLANG** (sound bridge from the THUD).

### SCENE 4 — THE WORLD AFTER ROCKY · 00:27.0–00:39.0 · Rapid montage

**SHOT 9 · 00:27.0–00:29.0 (2.0 s) — KGF**
The abandoned gold mine. Broken gates, silent shafts, wind blowing dust.
NARRATOR (0:27.3–0:28.9): *"ഖനികൾ നിശ്ശബ്ദമായി…"*

**SHOT 10 · 00:29.0–00:31.0 (2.0 s) — KGF settlement**
Old workers sit under a faded painted portrait of Rocky. A small boy stares at the distant mine.

**SHOT 11 · 00:31.0–00:33.5 (2.5 s) — Secret warehouse**
Five men around a table under one hanging bulb. One drops a gold ring engraved **R**, tied to a paper salvage tag that reads **RECOVERED — WRECK SITE**. Whisky glasses go up.
NARRATOR (0:31.5–0:33.2): *"ശത്രുക്കൾ ആഘോഷിച്ചു…"*

**SHOT 12 · 00:33.5–00:36.0 (2.5 s) — Foreign intelligence office**
A world map on a corkboard, with 16 countries joined by red string and pins. An officer opens a folder marked **CLASSIFIED — ROCKY**.

**SHOT 13 · 00:36.0–00:39.0 (3.0 s) — International cargo port**
A crane swings a crate. Close on a clipboard manifest: **OWNER: UNKNOWN**.
NARRATOR (pre-lap 0:35.6–0:38.8): *"പക്ഷേ… അവന്റെ സാമ്രാജ്യം ഉറങ്ങിയിരുന്നില്ല."*
**MUSIC HARD STOP at 0:39.0.**

### SCENE 5 — THE PHONE CALL · 00:39.0–00:49.0 · Delhi · Midnight

**SHOT 14 · 00:39.0–00:41.5 (2.5 s)**
A dark office lit by one desk lamp. 0.6 s of dead silence, then the red phone rings: **TRRRING** (0:39.6), **TRRRING** (0:40.4). Ramika's hand lifts the receiver (0:41.1).

**SHOT 15 · 00:41.5–00:44.5 (3.0 s)**
The officer in a dim records room, phone to his ear, sweat on his forehead.
OFFICER (0:41.8–0:44.3): *"Madam… the accounts in sixteen countries…"*

**SHOT 16 · 00:44.5–00:46.0 (1.5 s)**
Tight close-up of Ramika, half her face in shadow.
RAMIKA SEN (0:44.8–0:45.7): *"What about them?"*

**SHOT 17 · 00:46.0–00:49.0 (3.0 s)**
Extreme close-up of a telex printout under the officer's trembling finger: **LAST TRANSACTION: YESTERDAY**.
OFFICER (whisper, off-screen, 0:47.0–0:48.0): *"They're active again."*
**COMPLETE SILENCE** until 0:49.0.

### SCENE 6 — THE RETURN OF ROCKY · 00:49.0–01:00.0 · Unknown foreign harbour

**SHOT 18 · 00:49.0–00:51.5 (2.5 s)**
A snow-covered foreign harbour at dawn. A giant ship's horn blasts (0:49.0). A man's silhouette in the fog.
NARRATOR (0:49.6–0:52.0): *"അവർ അവനെ തിരഞ്ഞത്… കടലിലായിരുന്നു."*

**SHOT 19 · 00:51.5–00:54.5 (3.0 s)**
Extreme low angle. Black boots crunch on snow. The camera tilts up a long black coat to his right hand, wearing **the same gold R ring**.
NARRATOR (0:52.4–0:54.5): *"അവൻ കടന്നത്… അതിരുകളായിരുന്നു!"*

ROCKY (calm, deep): *"ഇനി…"* (0:55.4–0:55.8) … *"ലോകം."* (0:56.2–0:56.7)
**All sound drops out, 0:56.7–0:57.0.**

**SHOT 21 · 00:57.0–01:00.0 (3.0 s)**
Hard cut to black. **BOOM** (0:57.0). An original orchestral hit.
Title lockup slams in: **K.G.F** (0:57.0) → **CHAPTER 3** (0:57.4) → **THE GLOBAL EMPIRE** (0:57.8).
**UNOFFICIAL FAN CONCEPT** fades in small at the bottom (0:58.5). Fade to black 0:59.7–1:00.0.
