**Format:** 16:9 master (1920×1080) with a 2.39:1 letterbox: the active image is 1920×804, with 138 px black bars top and bottom.
**Composition rule:** keep every important element inside the central 2.39 band. Generate at 16:9 and crop in the edit (see Part 7).
