## 7.2 How to assemble it (DaVinci Resolve; Premiere and CapCut work the same way)

### Project setup
1. Timeline: **1920×1080, 24 fps**, Rec.709. Audio at 48 kHz.
2. Build the letterbox once on the top video track: an adjustment layer or **Output Blanking** (Resolve: Timeline → Output Blanking → 2.39). In Premiere or CapCut, use two black solids of 1920×138 px, top and bottom.
3. Track layout:

| Track | Content |
|---|---|
| V4 | Letterbox bars (top, locked) |
| V3 | Text and overlays (typewriter text, CLOSED, OWNER: UNKNOWN, telex line, title) |
| V2 | Grain + light leaks (optional, Overlay blend at 10–15%) |
| V1 | Video clips 1 → 21 |
| A1 | Dialogue / VO (L1–L16) |
| A2 | SFX: hard sync (THUD, CLANG, phone, horn, BOOM) |
| A3 | SFX: ambience beds (storm, room tone, wind) |
| A4 | Music: Cue A |
| A5 | Music: Cue B |
| A6 | Music: Cue C + BOOM hit |

### Step-by-step
1. **Lay the audio first.** Place L1–L16 at the exact timestamps in Part 5, then the hard SFX (S13, S14, S22, S26, S32). Audio is the skeleton; cut the picture to it.
2. **Drop in the clips.** Place each generated clip on V1 and trim it to the "Use" window from Part 4.2. Snap the cut points to the times in 7.1.
3. **Time-fit with retiming, not stretching.** If a clip is short, slow it to 85–95% with **optical flow** (Resolve: Retime Process → Optical Flow). Never go below 80%. If it's long, trim it.
4. **Lip-sync shots (7, 15, 16, 20).** Export each trimmed clip with its exact line, run it through the lip-sync tool, and re-import the result *over* the original. Check sync frame by frame on hard consonants.
5. **Text overlays (V3).**
   - Shot 1: typewriter text, revealed one character every 2 frames, Courier Prime, off-white, slight jitter.
   - Shots 8, 13, 17: if the AI text is wrong, use **planar tracking** (Resolve Fusion: Planar Tracker; After Effects: Mocha) to pin a clean text layer onto the prop.
   - Shot 21 title: use a serif or heavy display font (Cinzel, Trajan-style) with a gold gradient (#E7C27A → #8A6420), bevel/emboss at 15%, outer glow at 20%. Each line scales from 110% to 100% over 6 frames with a 2-frame white flash. "UNOFFICIAL FAN CONCEPT": a small sans-serif, 40% opacity, bottom centre, inside the letterbox band.
6. **Colour (one pass on all clips, then match).**
   - Node 1: normalise exposure and white balance for each clip.
   - Node 2: a global look of crushed blacks (lift −0.03), warm highlights (gain toward gold), cool shadows (lift toward blue), saturation 85%.
   - Node 3: per-scene tint. Scene 1 cold blue; Scene 2 warm amber; Scenes 3 and 5 split cold blue and tungsten; Scene 4 sepia/sodium; Scene 6 ice blue with a gold accent on Rocky's face and ring (a power window).
   - Shot match: use the Rocky reveal (20) as the hero reference, and match 18 and 19 to it.
   - Finish with film grain (35 mm, 25%) and a gentle vignette (15%) on everything.
7. **Transitions.** Almost all are **hard cuts**. The only exceptions are the fade-in at 0:00, the cut to black at 0:10, the sound bridge at 26, and the fade-out at 59.7. Don't use dissolves, whip pans or glitch effects: they cheapen it.
8. **Audio mix.** Dialogue −12 to −14 dBFS RMS. Duck the music with sidechain or keyframes, −10 dB under every line. Automate the **three silences** to true silence (mute every track, reverb returns included). Master to −14 LUFS / −1 dBTP.
9. **QC checklist before export.**
   - [ ] Duration is exactly 60:00 (1440 frames at 24 fps)
   - [ ] No face drift between Rocky's shots 18 → 19 → 20; the ring is on the right index finger every time
   - [ ] Ramika's shawl is on her left shoulder and her bangle on her right wrist in 6, 7, 8, 14 and 16
   - [ ] No garbled AI text visible anywhere
   - [ ] No modern objects in frame
   - [ ] The silences at 0:39, 0:48 and 0:56.7 are fully silent
   - [ ] BOOM on the exact frame of the cut to black (frame 1368)
   - [ ] "UNOFFICIAL FAN CONCEPT" is legible
10. **Export.** H.264 or H.265, 1920×1080, 24 fps, 20–30 Mbps, AAC 320 kbps. For Instagram Reels or Shorts, make a separate 9:16 reframe. Don't just letterbox the 16:9.

## 7.3 Production order (the fastest path)

1. Generate the character sheets and props (Part 3.1), then lock them.
2. Generate all VO (Part 5), then build a **radio-play animatic**: audio plus the still start frames on the timeline. Check the 60 s pacing *before* spending video credits.
3. Generate the video clips in this order: hero shots first (20, 19, 18, 7, 2A), then the rest.
4. Do the lip-sync passes.
5. Generate the music cues and SFX, then do a rough mix.
6. Text, colour, final mix, QC, export.
